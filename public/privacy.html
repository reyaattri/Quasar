PRIVACY POLICY

Your learning stays yours.

Effective September 22, 2026

Quasar is a visual learning and memory app. You can use the core learning experience without creating an account.

Information we handle

  • On-device learning data: lesson progress, review history, preferences, and profile details you choose to enter.
  • Account data: if cloud accounts are configured and you create one, Quasar processes your email address and encrypted authentication credentials through Supabase.
  • Cloud learning data: signed-in users may choose to back up learning progress and personalization fields.
  • Optional analytics: anonymous learning events are sent only when you enable the analytics setting. Personal answers and story text are excluded.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat and the applicable app store process subscription status and transaction data. Quasar does not receive full payment-card details.
  • Microphone: Korean speaking practice requests microphone access only when you start recording. Recordings remain in the practice session and are not uploaded by Quasar.

Personalized mnemonics

If you request a personalized mnemonic, the selected learning fact and the profile details shown in Settings are sent to the configured generation service to create the response. This only occurs after you press the personalization button.

Control and deletion

You can turn optional analytics off, reset device progress, sign out, or permanently delete a connected account from Settings. Account deletion removes the authentication record and linked cloud learning rows. App-store subscriptions must be cancelled separately in the store.

Security and retention

Cloud rows use per-user access rules. Local data remains until it is reset, the app is removed, or browser storage is cleared. Cloud data remains until the account is deleted.

Children

Quasar is an educational tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children without the authorization required by local law.
