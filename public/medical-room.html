INTERACTIVE PPG LAB · SIMULATED FOR LEARNING

Change one variable. Watch the pulse respond.

Photoplethysmography (PPG) estimates changing blood volume from light returning to a sensor. Site, wavelength, heart rate, age, contact, and motion all affect a real signal.

This is a qualitative teaching simulation, not a calibrated sensor or clinical signal. It shows expected direction of change rather than patient physiology.

Reveal the explanation
