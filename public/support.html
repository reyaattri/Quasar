SUPPORT

Let’s get you unstuck.

Most issues can be resolved from Settings: back up progress, restore a purchase, sign out, reset local progress, or delete a connected account.

When asking for help

Include your device model, operating-system version, the screen where the issue occurred, and the steps that reproduce it. Never send your password, authentication code, or payment information.

Account deletion

Open Settings → Your account → Delete account and cloud data. If you cannot access the app, open an issue in the repository once it is public and identify the account email without including passwords or codes.

Subscriptions

Purchases and cancellations are managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Use Restore Purchases inside Quasar after reinstalling.
