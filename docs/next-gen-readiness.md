## Project description draft

Quasar helps students remember what they learn, and prove it to themselves. Difficult ideas get a vivid place to live: illustrated scenes, walkable memory palaces and strange, specific cues. Then the app makes the learner work without the picture. They recall concepts with the image hidden, **teach them back** in their own words (Quasar asks a guiding question about whatever they left out instead of handing over the answer), climb a five-rung **Why Ladder** from *what happens* to *use it somewhere new*, and solve original field cases.

Every attempt feeds a learning history on the device. When a concept slips twice, **Error Memory** shows the exact wrong answer the learner keeps choosing next to what's true, offers other ways to see it, and only clears after a correct unaided answer. **Today** turns that history into a short plan that fits 5, 15 or 30 minutes, ranked by knowledge gaps, due reviews, exam date, importance and prerequisites, with a reason shown for every task. **Ready** measures factual recall, conceptual understanding and unfamiliar problem-solving separately, using only answers given without help.

The core lessons need no account and aren't paywalled. Quasar Plus, through RevenueCat, adds personalized mnemonic stories built around the learner's interests.
