# Korean illustration refresh

Three transparent generated illustrations replace the rejected eye-based cues and café scene. Natural proportions, hanbok and restrained ink/gouache distinguish the new art from childish cartoons. Korean vocabulary remains Korean; visual associations are not presented as etymology.

## child

Asset: assets/korean-word-child.png

Prompt: Refined Korean traditional editorial illustration inspired by Joseon genre painting and contemporary Korean book illustration. Elegant restrained ink outlines, subtle flat gouache, muted indigo, warm ivory, soft coral and jade. Natural human proportions, small realistic faces, adults look adult, dignified posture, sophisticated not childish, NOT chibi, NOT kawaii, NOT Disney, no huge heads or huge eyes, no glossy 3D. A naturally proportioned Korean child flying a traditional rectangular bangpae kite, hanbok moving gently in the wind. Subtle delight. No text on kite. Transparent background, isolated complete vignette with generous margins, no rectangular backdrop, no text, no watermark. Clear and warm, slight gentle humor.
