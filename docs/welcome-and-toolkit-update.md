## Final image prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Transparent PNG decorative artwork for the TOP EDGE of a mobile learning app's dark forest green progress card. Four distinct delicate flowering stems hanging straight down from the top edge like a botanical mobile, with thin nearly vertical strings/stems, small cream jasmine flowers and muted blush bells and sage leaves. Stems start at top, hang to varying lengths and stop by upper third. Spread across the width with generous space between, keep the central lower two thirds entirely empty transparent for app text. No arches, no curved horizontal garlands, no corner vines, no frames, no text, no other background. Handmade gouache and fine pencil, subtle elegant detail, restrained warm colours. Wide horizontal composition about 3:1.
